Status: Actual ChatGPT trials and retest completed; AI-assisted report for student review. No banking system was deployed or coursework submitted.

## Governing question and scenario
How much creative destruction could employee-assisted, tool-using AI cause in a retail bank's complaints business model, on what horizon, and what should management do now?

The primary case is a hypothetical medium-to-large US retail bank considering assistance with web-submitted credit-card billing disputes: understanding case narratives, gathering approved records and policy evidence, suggesting routing/escalation and drafting responses. Employees retain final authority. No actual bank data, customer outcomes or system access were used. The specialist built for this assignment evaluates the business implications of that proposed application.

## Three levels of finding
General technology: Tool-using AI can support information gathering and analysis; the technology label alone does not establish disruption, quality or economic value [S4].

Application: Complaint investigation and drafting may become less dependent on repeated manual lookup and administrative handoffs. Benefits depend on correct evidence, human review and actual resolution. The relevant real-world example is bank-reported testing [S2], with historical failure patterns motivating case tests [S3].

Organization: For the hypothetical bank, provisional potency is Moderate, primarily affecting a service/cost workflow. The same technology poses a stronger conditional revenue threat to a provider paid for human handling hours. Integration constraints change local readiness, not general technology capability.

## Business-model baseline
The bank offers financial services to retail consumers through digital and human service channels. Financial products and relationships support revenue; complaint handling protects service quality and trust while consuming investigation, review and communication effort. Relevant capabilities are experienced handlers, controlled case records, reliable policy interpretation and accountable judgment. Staff and technology/service partners support the workflow. These are teaching-case assumptions, not verified facts about a named institution.

## Creative destruction ledger
Create or enable: structured evidence gathering; clearer case summaries; assistance with routing and response drafting; feedback on recurring complaint causes; staff capacity for difficult investigation.

Displace or reduce: duplicate entry, routine lookup, standard drafting and some administrative handoffs; potentially purchased manual handling hours. Policy expertise and accountable case judgment remain complementary. A new review or quality-assurance burden could offset time savings.

The causal mechanism is substituting assisted organization of unstructured text and approved evidence for selected repetitive work. It does not by itself change the bank's financial product or revenue model. Bank-wide existential disruption is unsupported.

## Potency and matrix placement
Business-Model Disruption Potency: Moderate, provisional, for the complaints operating model. Matrix: Moderate severity and near-term material workflow impact under the primary case's readiness assumption. Figure 1.8 recommends Pilot or partner [S1]. The draft follows that action rather than recommending unrestricted automation.

Planning bins used for testing are near within 12 months, mid 12-36 months and far beyond 36 months. These are analyst-defined scenario bins, not textbook definitions or measured forecasts. The proposed 90-day pilot is a learning period, not a promise of enterprise transformation.

## Incumbent dilemma and bounded response
Management may need to reconsider routines, handling-based incentives, administrative roles and outsourcing arrangements while preserving investigative expertise. Acting early risks adding review/rework cost and harming service through unsupported conclusions. Waiting risks preserving inefficient arrangements and losing learning time if competitors improve service.

The complaints operations manager should sponsor a proposed 90-day employee-assisted pilot with risk and data owners. Start with approved de-identified retrospective cases; progress to live assistance only after authorization and access/audit readiness. Compare similar case types against human-only handling. Measure total resolution cost including reviewer effort and rework, correct escalation, unsupported statements, resolution time and repeated complaints. Set criteria before testing. Pause affected use after unauthorized actions, unsupported customer-impacting decisions or worsening outcomes. Do not approve staffing cuts from these examples.

One urgency trigger is independently checked evidence that a relevant competitor or supplier achieves sustained improvements in both total cost and customer outcomes. It would justify reassessing timing and competitive exposure; faster answer generation alone would not.

## Context comparison and actual runtime evidence
Primary bank: Moderate potency; Moderate/near; Pilot or partner. Its complaints process is a service/cost activity.

Contrast 1 labor-priced supplier: High conditional potency; High/mid; Prepare and pilot. Its billing-hour revenue could erode if clients need less manual work. Timing depends on contracts and adoption. Alternative pricing and specialized quality services are possibilities to test.

Contrast 2 readiness-constrained bank: Moderate conditional potency; Moderate/mid for local material workflow impact; Monitor and build optionality. Approved access, source lineage and audit ownership must be established. External competitive risk may still be near-term.

Stable substantive finding across actual runs: plausible assistance with evidence gathering and drafting, but no demonstrated net outcomes or production readiness. Changed finding: the exposed economic unit, potency, impact horizon and management response vary with labor-hour pricing and local readiness. The general finding is substantively stable rather than identical text. These are three revised context runs, not a statistical estimate of reliability.

## Limits and team handoff
The bank and supplier assumptions are hypothetical; costs, quality, customer response and commercial viability are unmeasured. The strongest inference is that identical technology can affect a bank cost workflow and a supplier revenue model differently. The most exposed bank activities are routine evidence gathering and administrative processing. Preserve the conditional matrix placement, employee authority, lack of bank-specific economics and limitations of these small actual trials when integrating this specialist.
