# Independent professional judgment draft

Student review and endorsement required. The body is intended to fit within half a page.

My provisional judgment is that employee-assisted AI complaint handling presents Moderate disruption potential for a retail bank's operating model. Its principal mechanism is reducing routine information gathering and drafting effort while improving the organization of case evidence. These changes can reshape tasks and supplier demand, but the evidence does not show that this application threatens the bank's core revenue model. I therefore regard it primarily as a sustaining service improvement with creative destruction of selected work.

Under the hypothetical bank's readiness assumptions, I place the material workflow impact at Moderate severity and near-term, supporting a bounded pilot or partnership. I recommend a proposed 90-day employee-assisted pilot with approved data access, source traceability, audit records and final human authority. The decision should depend on actual resolution quality and total cost, including review and rework, rather than response speed alone. I would reassess urgency if independently checked competitor or supplier results demonstrate sustained advantages in both cost and customer outcomes. Bank-specific economics, deployment readiness and agent reliability remain unverified.
